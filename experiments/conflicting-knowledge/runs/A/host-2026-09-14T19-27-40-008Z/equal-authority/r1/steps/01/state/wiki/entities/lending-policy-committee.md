The **approving authority** for Northstar Standard Home Loan policy. Responsible for approving, amending, and clarifying lending policy documents.

Key governance principles tied to this committee:
- Only the committee can approve changes to policy.
- Informal team notes or draft discussions cannot override approved policy.
- Conflicts between equally authoritative documents must be resolved by the committee, not by staff.
