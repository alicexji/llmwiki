Active operating standard for production incident response (Source Type: Operational Policy; Department: IT Operations & SRE; Status: Approved; Published: November 10, 2024; Effective: December 1, 2024). For P1 critical outages: Incident Commander must verbally page and brief the VP of Engineering within fifteen (15) minutes, mobilize a minimum of 5 Tier-3 engineers, and send executive email status updates every thirty (30) minutes.
