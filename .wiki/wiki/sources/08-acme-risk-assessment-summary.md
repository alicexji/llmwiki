This brief document reports the outcome of a **Risk team assessment of Acme** conducted ahead of the planned Atlas rollout. The assessment identifies Acme as presenting **elevated account risk**, citing recent adverse changes in the customer's financial position and several unresolved contractual dependencies that could increase the likelihood of disruption during a large-scale deployment.

Based on these findings, the Risk team recommends a cautious approach: limiting initial exposure, closely monitoring the account, and establishing clear escalation criteria before expanding the relationship further. Importantly, the report clarifies that these concerns do not block the planned Atlas deployment outright, but should factor into decisions about the rollout's size and pace.
