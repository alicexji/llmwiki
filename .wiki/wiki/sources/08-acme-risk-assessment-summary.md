This brief risk assessment from Acme's Risk team evaluates account health ahead of the planned Atlas rollout. The team identifies elevated account risk driven by recent changes in Acme's financial position and unresolved contractual dependencies, both of which increase the likelihood of disruption during a large-scale deployment.

The Risk team's recommendations are cautious but not blocking: they advise limiting initial exposure, closely monitoring the account, and establishing clear escalation criteria before expanding the relationship further. Importantly, these concerns are not framed as a reason to halt the Atlas deployment, but rather as factors that should inform the size and pace of the rollout to Acme.
