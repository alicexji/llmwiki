## Overview
During August, the Product team saw an increase in customer feedback related to **Project Atlas**, driven by expanded pilot usage.

### Key Issues Reported
- **Performance**: Pilot users reported slower page loads during heavy usage, especially when loading large loan scenarios (related to the Commercial Lending Platform).
- **Support tickets**: Volume increased over July, with common topics being navigation confusion, login difficulties, and slow page loads.
- **Root cause**: Not yet identified as of this report.

### Follow-up
Product asked Engineering to continue monitoring application performance as the pilot expands.
