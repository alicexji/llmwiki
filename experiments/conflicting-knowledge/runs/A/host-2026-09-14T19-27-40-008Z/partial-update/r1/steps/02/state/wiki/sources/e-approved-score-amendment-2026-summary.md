This document (Document ID: E) is an approved amendment to the Northstar Standard Home Loan policy (Policy A), specifically targeting the minimum credit score requirement. Approved by the Lending Policy Committee, it was published on 2026-01-15 and becomes effective 2026-02-01. The amendment is narrow in scope: it changes only the minimum credit score, raising it to 700, and explicitly does not supersede or otherwise alter Policy A as a whole.

The amendment applies to the same scope as Policy A — owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. No changes are made to loan-to-value requirements, maximum loan term, or any other numeric limits in Policy A; these remain fully in force. Staff are instructed to read this amendment alongside Policy A to determine the complete set of applicable requirements, and the document stresses that omission of other limits from the amendment text does not cancel them.

A key governance point is the distinction between publication and effect: publication provides advance notice, but the amended credit score requirement only becomes binding from the stated effective date (2026-02-01), not the publication date.
