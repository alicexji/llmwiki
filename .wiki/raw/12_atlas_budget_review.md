# Atlas Q3 Budget Review

Project Atlas spending exceeded the Q3 forecast by approximately 8%.

The largest areas of spending during the quarter were engineering labor, cloud infrastructure, and external services.

Cloud infrastructure spending increased as the Atlas pilot expanded to additional users and environments.

The project also incurred planned costs associated with database migration work and performance testing.

Finance and Platform Engineering are reviewing the remaining 2026 forecast.

No single initiative has been identified as the primary cause of the Q3 variance.
