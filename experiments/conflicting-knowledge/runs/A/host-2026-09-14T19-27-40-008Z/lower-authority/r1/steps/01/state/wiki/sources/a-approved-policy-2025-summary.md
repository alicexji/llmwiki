This document defines the **approved 2025 lending policy** (Document ID: A) for the Northstar Standard Home Loan, an owner-occupied purchase loan product for standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of Northland. It was approved by the **Lending Policy Committee** on 2024-12-15, with an effective date of 2025-01-01, and does not supersede any prior document.

Key lending parameters are specified: maximum **loan-to-value ratio (LTV)** of 80%, maximum loan term of 30 years, and minimum credit score of 680. These requirements apply specifically to owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers, denominated in Northland dollars.

The policy also establishes governance rules for how it can be changed or interpreted: only the Lending Policy Committee can approve changes; informal notes or drafts cannot override approved policy; replacement documents take effect on their stated effective date rather than publication date; amendments only modify the specific requirements named, leaving others intact; and in cases of conflicting equally-authoritative requirements with no clear precedence, the matter remains unresolved and staff must not invent solutions.
