# Atlas Service Incident — August 12

Atlas experienced a production service interruption on August 12 between 2:14 PM and 3:01 PM.

During the incident, some users were unable to submit new loan scenarios. Existing saved scenarios remained accessible.

Platform Engineering restored normal service at 3:01 PM.

The incident investigation remains open. The engineering team has not yet identified a confirmed root cause.

Additional monitoring has been enabled while the investigation continues.
