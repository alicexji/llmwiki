# Acme Account Review

The Sales team reviewed the Acme relationship as part of planning for the Atlas launch.

Acme is expected to be one of the most strategically important customers in the initial Atlas rollout. The customer has expressed interest in expanding usage across multiple business units if the first deployment is successful.

Sales recommends prioritizing Acme during launch planning and assigning dedicated support during onboarding. A successful deployment could create a significant expansion opportunity over the next year.

The account team will continue working with Acme on rollout planning.
