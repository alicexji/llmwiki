## Overview
A status note on **Project Atlas** funding, produced jointly by **Finance** and **Platform Engineering**.

**Key points:**
- Current funding is sufficient to continue planned development and pilot activities through the current quarter.
- The 2027 funding request will be reviewed by leadership during the annual planning process.
- No final 2027 budget has been approved yet.
- No specific funding amounts are disclosed in this update.

This update complements earlier budget reviews (e.g., the Atlas Q3 Budget Review) by addressing forward-looking funding continuity rather than historical spend.
