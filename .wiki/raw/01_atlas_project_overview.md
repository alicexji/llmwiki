# Project Atlas Overview

Project Atlas is Northstar Financial's initiative to modernize its commercial lending platform.

The project began in January 2026 and is being developed primarily by the Platform Engineering team. Maya Chen, Director of Platform Engineering, oversees the team responsible for Atlas.

Atlas is intended to replace several legacy lending services with a unified platform for loan processing, pricing, and risk analysis.

The current target is to make Atlas generally available to internal users in October 2026.

The Data Engineering team maintains several shared data pipelines used by Atlas and other Northstar applications.

The project also depends on authentication services provided by external vendor AuthCore.
