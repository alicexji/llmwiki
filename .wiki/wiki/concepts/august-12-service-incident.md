## Overview
A production service interruption affecting **Project Atlas** occurred on **August 12** between 2:14 PM and 3:01 PM (47 minutes).

## Impact
- Some users unable to submit new loan scenarios.
- Existing saved scenarios remained accessible (partial degradation).

## Resolution
- **Platform Engineering** restored normal service at 3:01 PM.
- Root cause not yet confirmed at time of this report.
- Additional monitoring enabled pending investigation.

This incident is later followed up by a root-cause investigation.
