A loan product for **owner-occupied purchase loans** targeting standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of **Northland**, denominated in Northland dollars.

**2025 Approved Policy Parameters (Document ID: A):**
- Maximum LTV: 80%
- Maximum loan term: 30 years
- Minimum credit score: 680

Approved by the Lending Policy Committee, effective 2025-01-01.
