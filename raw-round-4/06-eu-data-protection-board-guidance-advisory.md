# Regulatory Compliance Notice: Cross-Border Transfers of Pseudonymized Telemetry (REG-ADV-2026-09)

Issued by the Chief Compliance Officer and European Data Protection Liaison, summarizing the binding enforcement guidelines published by the European Data Protection Board (EDPB).

## 1. Regulatory Context & Jurisprudence
Recent formal determinations by European supervisory authorities clarify the legal interpretation of pseudonymization under General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Article 4(5) and Chapter V transfer restrictions.

## 2. Key Binding Compliance Directives
- **Pseudonymization Is Not Anonymization:** Data subjected to salted cryptographic hashing or tokenization remains legally personal data so long as the original identifiers can be linked via supplementary keys or contextual re-identification.
- **Prohibition on Unilateral Cross-Border Egress:** Transferring pseudonymous telemetry concerning European residents to third-country servers (including US-based cloud infrastructure) without formal Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), Supplementary Technical Measures (end-to-end client-side encryption), and an executed Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA) constitutes an actionable statutory violation.
- **Immediate Regulatory Requirement:** All autonomous replication of European telemetry pipelines into external geographic partitions must be suspended immediately pending completion of localized regional processing safeguards.
