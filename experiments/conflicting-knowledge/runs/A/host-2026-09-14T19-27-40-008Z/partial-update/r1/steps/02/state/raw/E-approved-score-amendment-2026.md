# Northstar Standard Home Loan: approved credit score amendment

Document ID: E. This amendment was approved by the Lending Policy Committee. Its status is approved. It was published on 2026-01-15 and takes effect on 2026-02-01. It amends only the minimum credit score requirement in policy A; it does not supersede policy A as a whole.

The amendment applies to Northstar Standard Home Loan owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. There is no change to the product, jurisdiction or borrower class covered by policy A.

From the effective date, the minimum credit score is 700. This replaces the prior minimum credit score requirement in policy A for the same scope. All other requirements in policy A remain unchanged and in force. In particular, this amendment does not change the loan-to-value requirement or the maximum loan term.

Staff must read this amendment together with policy A to determine the full applicable requirements. The omission of other numeric limits from this amendment does not cancel those limits. Publication gives advance notice; the amended score requirement applies only from the stated effective date.
