A set of governance rules for interpreting and applying lending policy documents:

- **Replacement policies** take effect on their stated *effective date*, not their publication date.
- **Amendments** only change the specific requirements they name; all other requirements remain in force.
- If two equally authoritative documents have the **same date** and state **incompatible requirements**, with no established precedence, the conflict is **unresolved** pending committee clarification.
- Staff must **not** invent exceptions or choose values to resolve such conflicts unilaterally.

These rules ensure consistent, auditable governance over lending policy documents like the Northstar Standard Home Loan policy.
