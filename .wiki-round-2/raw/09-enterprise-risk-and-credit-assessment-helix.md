# Credit Risk & Counterparty Assessment: Helix Logistics

Prepared in late May 2026 by Devon Thorne, Head of Risk Management, for the Risk Management and Underwriting Committee regarding counterparty exposure and settlement underwriting guardrails for Helix Logistics on Project Meridian.

## Risk Evaluation & Underwriting Analysis
The Risk Management and Underwriting Committee has completed an evaluation of Helix Logistics prior to extending real-time settlement clearing privileges under Project Meridian.

## Key Risk Indicators
1. **Leverage & Debt-to-Equity Ratio:** Helix currently carries a debt-to-equity ratio of 3.8x following aggressive debt-financed acquisitions in 2024–2025, significantly higher than industry median benchmarks (1.9x).
2. **Fuel Price Sensitivity & Operating Margin Compression:** Helix experienced an 18% year-over-year contraction in operating margin due to volatile maritime fuel surcharges and reduced ocean freight spot rates.
3. **Cross-Border Regulatory Scrutiny:** The entity is currently subject to an ongoing customs audit by European authorities regarding declared invoice valuations in two regional subsidiaries.

## Risk Committee Recommendation & Guardrails
Because real-time settlement provides instant liquidity with compressed reconciliation windows, extending uncollateralized clearing limits presents significant insolvency exposure:
1. **Mandatory Cash Reserve Escrow:** Helix must deposit and maintain a rolling cash collateral reserve equal to 5 days of average daily settlement volume ($2.5M minimum).
2. **Daily Exposure Caps:** Implement hard automated intraday exposure caps of $1.2M on unreconciled transaction queues.
3. **Rejection of Fee Concessions:** Fee waivers or delayed billing terms are strictly prohibited from a risk governance standpoint until financial metrics improve for two consecutive quarters.
