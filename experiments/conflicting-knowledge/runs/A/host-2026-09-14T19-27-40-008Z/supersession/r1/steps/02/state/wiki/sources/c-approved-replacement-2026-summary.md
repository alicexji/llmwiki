This document (ID: C) is the approved 2026 replacement policy for the **Northstar Standard Home Loan**, issued by the **Lending Policy Committee**. It was published on 2026-02-15 and takes effect 2026-03-01, explicitly superseding policy A on that same date. The scope remains unchanged from policy A: owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars.

Key approved requirements effective 2026-03-01: maximum LTV of 82%, maximum loan term of 30 years, and minimum credit score of 680.

The document emphasizes precedence rules: publication merely gives notice of a forthcoming replacement and does not bring new requirements into effect early. Policy A continues to govern until the effective date, after which it becomes historical evidence only. Informal team statements are explicitly not approved policy.
