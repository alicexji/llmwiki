This brief document restates the status of Atlas general availability (GA) planning. The team is targeting **October 2026** for GA to internal users, but this is explicitly framed as a planning target, not a committed release date. The final release date is contingent on completion of readiness activities and a leadership review process. The document notes that a confirmed release date will be provided only after readiness activities conclude.

This source contains no new entities and largely reinforces the existing October Release / Atlas Readiness Review concepts already present in the wiki, adding a specific target year (2026) and clarifying the conditional, non-committed nature of the timeline.
