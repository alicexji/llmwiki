## Overview
On **August 10**, the Atlas engineering team completed a production migration of the primary transactional database from **MySQL** to **PostgreSQL**.

## Details
- Planned over approximately **three months**.
- Goal: improve **scalability** to handle increasing transaction volume.
- Post-migration validation showed primary Atlas services operating normally.
- Engineering continued monitoring database performance for several weeks afterward.
- Considered one of the **largest infrastructure changes** completed by the Atlas team in the quarter.
