**Atlas External Availability** describes the current release status of Project Atlas as of this note: it is restricted to an internal pilot for approved Northstar Financial employees, with no external customer release. The pilot is meant to collect feedback before a wider internal rollout. External release has not been approved or scheduled, and any future external launch would require additional product, compliance, and security review.
