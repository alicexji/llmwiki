This short status note reports that the Security team has completed its **initial review** of Project Atlas. Several recommendations arising from that review have been passed to the engineering team and are being incorporated into ongoing readiness work. The document explicitly clarifies that completing the initial review is not equivalent to final security approval for general availability (GA), and that final readiness will only be assessed once the remaining recommendations have been implemented. As of this note, Atlas has **not** received final security approval for GA.

The document is notable for its careful distinction between an initial review milestone and final approval, avoiding any implication that security clearance for launch has been granted.
