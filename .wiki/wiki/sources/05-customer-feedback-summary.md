This source summarizes customer feedback related to Project Atlas gathered during August, based on input from pilot users. The primary theme is performance concerns: several pilot users reported slower page loads during periods of heavy usage, particularly when loading large loan scenarios.

Support ticket volume also increased compared to July, with common topics including navigation questions, login difficulties, and slow page loads. The Product team has asked Engineering to continue monitoring application performance as the pilot expands. Notably, no single root cause has been identified for the increase in support requests, suggesting the issues may stem from multiple contributing factors rather than one clear defect.
