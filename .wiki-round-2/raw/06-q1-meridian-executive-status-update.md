# Project Meridian: Q1 2026 Executive Status Update

Prepared by Lead Product Manager Jordan Hayes for executive leadership and program stakeholders covering the Q1 period from January through March 2026. The program is operating smoothly within healthy green status parameters.

During Q1 2026, the Project Meridian team achieved all primary milestones, successfully concluding the architectural definition phase and standing up our baseline containerized infrastructure on AWS.
