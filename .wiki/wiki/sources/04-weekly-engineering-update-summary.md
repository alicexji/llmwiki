This weekly update from the Platform Engineering team recaps a busy week for Project Atlas, covering three major threads: completion of the PostgreSQL migration, a production incident, and continued release preparation. On Monday, engineering finished the planned PostgreSQL migration with successful initial validation, marking a milestone tied to the broader database modernization effort. On Wednesday, the team responded to a 47-minute production service interruption affecting loan scenario submissions, which aligns with the previously documented August service incident.

Following the incident, engineers spent the rest of the week conducting root-cause style review work—examining application logs, database metrics, and service dependencies—suggesting a thorough post-incident investigation process. The team also continued preparing Atlas for its planned October release, indicating parallel workstreams between stabilization efforts and forward progress on new features.

Maya Chen is noted as thanking the engineering team for their quick incident response, reinforcing her leadership role over the Atlas/Platform Engineering effort.
