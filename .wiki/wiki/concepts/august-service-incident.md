## Overview
A production service interruption affecting Project Atlas occurred on **August 12**, between **2:14 PM and 3:01 PM**.

## Impact
- Some users were unable to submit new loan scenarios.
- Existing saved scenarios remained accessible during the incident.

## Resolution
Platform Engineering restored normal service at 3:01 PM.

## Status
The investigation remains open; no confirmed root cause has been identified. Additional monitoring has been enabled while the investigation continues.
