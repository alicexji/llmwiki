# Executive Exception Authorization: SRE Disaster Recovery Travel Protocol (EXEC-EXC-2026-04)

Authored by Rachel Sterling (Chief Operating Officer) with formal approval from the Executive Committee.

## 1. Context & Operational Necessity
During the multi-region data center infrastructure consolidation and migration program, critical hardware or networking failures require immediate, physically present engineering triage to prevent enterprise SLA breaches.

## 2. Authorized Exception Terms
To ensure immediate on-flight technical readiness, secure in-transit terminal access, and rested physical deployment upon landing:
- **Eligible Personnel:** Principal Site Reliability Engineers (SREs) and designated Infrastructure Disaster Recovery Strike Team members on active callout duty.
- **Flight Duration Threshold:** For any emergency deployment flight with duration exceeding **four (4) hours**, designated SRE personnel are explicitly authorized to book **Business Class** accommodations.
- **Billing & Account Allocation:** Incremental airfare expenses authorized under this exception shall be debited directly against the Executive Infrastructure Contingency budget rather than individual squad operational budgets.
- **Duration:** This exception remains in effect through the conclusion of the 2026 data center migration cycle.
