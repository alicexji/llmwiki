# Production Incident Classification and Escalation Policy (OPS-POL-2024-19)

Issued by the IT Operations Directorate and Site Reliability Engineering Leadership, formally approved as the active operating standard for production incident management.

## 1. Scope & Severity Definitions
This policy establishes standardized response cadences, communication protocols, and escalation paths for all production service disruptions impacting core customer platforms.

## 2. P1 (Critical Severity) Escalation Protocols
For all incidents designated as Priority 1 (P1 - Complete platform disruption, data loss risk, or systemic payment clearing failure):
- **Executive Escalation SLA:** The Incident Commander must directly page and verbally brief the Vice President of Engineering within **fifteen (15) minutes** of incident declaration.
- **Incident Swarm Size:** A minimum of five (5) designated Tier-3 engineers must be mobilized into the bridge immediately upon P1 initiation.
- **Executive Status Cadence:** Formal email briefings must be transmitted to the Executive Incident Distribution List every **thirty (30) minutes** throughout active mitigation.
- **Post-Incident Review:** A formal Post-Incident Review (PIR) document must be completed and circulated within forty-eight (48) hours of resolution.
