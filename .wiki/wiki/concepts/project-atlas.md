**Project Atlas** is Northstar Financial's initiative to modernize its commercial lending platform, begun in January 2026. It is intended to replace several legacy lending services with a unified platform for loan processing, pricing, and risk analysis. The current target for general internal availability is October 2026. Atlas depends on shared data pipelines maintained by Data Engineering and authentication services provided by AuthCore.
