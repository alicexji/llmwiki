An internal readiness review scheduled for **September 18** to assess Project Atlas's preparedness for general availability (GA).

**Prerequisites for GA:**
- Security review
- Performance testing
- Operational readiness checks

The Security team has provided recommendations on **session management** and **authentication logging**, which Engineering is reviewing. The final GA date will be confirmed after this review, keeping the October target tentative.
