**Maya Chen** is Director of Platform Engineering. Her organization includes the engineering team responsible for developing and operating **Project Atlas**. She participates in Atlas leadership reviews and provides engineering updates to technology leadership. She does not lead Atlas product strategy, and final Atlas release decisions are made through the readiness and leadership review process rather than by her individually.
