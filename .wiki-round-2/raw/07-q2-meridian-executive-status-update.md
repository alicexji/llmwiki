# Project Meridian: Q2 2026 Executive Status Update

Prepared by Lead Product Manager Jordan Hayes for executive leadership and program stakeholders covering the Q2 period from April through June 2026. Program tracking indicates a yellow advisory status due to external dependency shifts.

## Executive Summary
Q2 2026 delivered substantial engineering progress, including the rollout of our partner sandbox environment. However, unexpected delays during third-party SOC2 compliance certification and extended security penetration testing have impacted our production launch pipeline.

## Schedule Realignment
Due to external audit requirements and additional lead time required for regional banking gateway interconnects, the program timeline has been revised as follows:

- **Beta Partner Integration Sandbox:** July 20, 2026 (Completed)
- **Security & Compliance Sign-Off:** October 15, 2026
- **Revised General Availability (GA) Launch:** **November 18, 2026**

This shifts our GA target out of Q3 and into late Q4. Marcus Vance and Jordan Hayes have aligned with commercial leadership to ensure pilot partner launch agreements reflect the revised November 18 date.
