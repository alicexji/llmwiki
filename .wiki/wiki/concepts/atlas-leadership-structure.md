Defines the governance and decision-making structure for **Project Atlas**:

- **Platform Engineering** (led by Maya Chen) develops and operates Atlas, and provides engineering updates during leadership reviews.
- **Product Lending** owns product strategy for Atlas.
- **Release decisions** are made collectively via the Atlas readiness and leadership review process, not by any single individual.

This structure separates engineering execution, product strategy, and release governance into distinct accountable groups.
