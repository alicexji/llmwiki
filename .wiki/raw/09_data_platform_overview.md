# Enterprise Data Platform

Northstar's Data Engineering team operates the company's shared analytical data platform.

The team maintains ingestion pipelines that collect loan, pricing, and risk data from several internal systems.

Atlas consumes data from two of these shared pipelines for its risk analysis functionality.

The same pipelines are also used by the Portfolio Analytics and Forecasting applications.

Data Engineering is responsible for pipeline availability, schema management, and data quality monitoring.

Application teams remain responsible for the products that consume the shared data.
