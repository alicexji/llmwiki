This document defines the **approved 2025 policy** (Document ID: A) for the **Northstar Standard Home Loan**, an owner-occupied purchase loan product for standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of **Northland**, denominated in Northland dollars. The policy was approved by the **Lending Policy Committee** on 2024-12-15, with an effective date of 2025-01-01, and does not supersede any prior document.

Key lending parameters specified are: a maximum loan-to-value ratio (LTV) of **80%**, a maximum loan term of **30 years**, and a minimum credit score of **680**. These requirements apply uniformly to the specified product and borrower class.

The document also establishes **governance rules** for policy interpretation: only the Lending Policy Committee can approve changes; informal notes or drafts cannot override approved policy; replacements take effect on their stated effective date rather than publication date; amendments only alter the specific requirements they name, leaving others intact; and in cases of conflicting, equally authoritative documents with no established precedence, the conflict must remain unresolved pending committee clarification rather than being resolved unilaterally by staff.
