# Acme Risk Assessment

The Risk team completed its assessment of Acme ahead of the planned Atlas rollout.

Acme currently presents elevated account risk. Recent changes in the customer's financial position and several outstanding contractual dependencies increase the possibility of disruption during a large-scale deployment.

Risk recommends limiting initial exposure, monitoring the account closely, and establishing clear escalation criteria before expanding the relationship.

These concerns do not prevent the planned Atlas deployment, but they should be considered when determining the size and pace of the rollout.
