This concept covers the rules governing how lending policy documents are approved, amended, and superseded within the Northstar policy framework:

- Only the **Lending Policy Committee** can approve or change policy.
- **Informal notes or draft discussions** cannot alter an approved policy.
- A **replacement document** takes effect on its stated effective date, not its publication date.
- An **amendment** changes only the specific requirements it names; all other requirements remain in force.
- If **equally authoritative documents** with the same dates conflict and no precedence is established, the requirement is **unresolved** pending committee clarification — staff must not invent a resolution.

This framework ensures policy changes are traceable, authoritative, and unambiguous.
