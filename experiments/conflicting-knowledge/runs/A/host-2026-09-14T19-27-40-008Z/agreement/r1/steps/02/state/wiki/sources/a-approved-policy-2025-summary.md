This document is the **approved 2025 policy** (Document ID A) for the **Northstar Standard Home Loan**, an owner-occupied purchase loan product for standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of **Northland**, denominated in Northland dollars. It was approved by the **Lending Policy Committee** on 2024-12-15, with a status of approved and an effective date of 2025-01-01. It does not supersede any prior document.

Key lending parameters set by this policy: **maximum LTV of 80%**, **maximum loan term of 30 years**, and a **minimum credit score of 680**. These apply uniformly to the specified product and borrower class.

The policy also establishes governance rules for how future changes must be handled: only the Lending Policy Committee can approve changes; informal notes/drafts cannot override approved policy; explicit replacements take effect on their stated effective date (not publication date); amendments only change the specific requirements they name; and if two equally authoritative documents conflict without a clear precedence rule, the conflict remains unresolved and staff must not invent a resolution.
