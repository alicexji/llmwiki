This source summarizes customer feedback received during August related to the Atlas pilot. Several pilot users reported slower page performance during periods of heavy usage, specifically noting delays when loading large loan scenarios. Support tickets also increased compared to July, with common topics including navigation questions, login difficulties, and slow page loads.

The Product team has asked Engineering to continue monitoring application performance as the pilot expands. Notably, the document explicitly states that no single cause has been identified for the increase in support requests, indicating the feedback does not establish a specific root cause.
