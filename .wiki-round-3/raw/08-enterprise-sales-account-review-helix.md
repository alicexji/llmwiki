# Strategic Account Review: Helix Logistics

Prepared in mid-May 2026 by Sarah Lin, VP of Enterprise Sales, reviewing commercial positioning for Helix Logistics as a premier enterprise partner for the Project Meridian freight settlement rail.

## Commercial Opportunity Overview
Helix Logistics represents our most significant enterprise pipeline opportunity for FY2026. As a global freight forwarding and logistics conglomerate with operations across 40 countries, securing Helix as the flagship pilot customer for Project Meridian will establish our presence in the multi-trillion-dollar freight settlement ecosystem.

## Projected Value & Strategic Impact
- **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR):** Projected $4.2M in recurring software and platform usage fees within year one.
- **Transaction Volume:** Estimated 18M monthly settlement transactions, serving as an immediate high-volume validation of Meridian's real-time architecture.
- **Market Positioning:** Signing Helix creates a formidable barrier to entry for competing fintech providers in the European and North American freight corridors.

## Commercial Recommendation
To ensure rapid contract execution and guarantee their participation as the primary showcase partner at our Q4 launch:
1. Provide a 15% discount on variable per-transaction processing fees for the first 12 months.
2. Grant Helix priority access to engineering escalations and dedicated technical account management.
3. Waive upfront onboarding implementation fees to secure a 3-year exclusivity commitment.
