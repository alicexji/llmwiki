A loan product covered by the 2025 approved policy (Document ID: A). Applies to **owner-occupied purchase loans** for standard individual borrowers in **Northland**, denominated in Northland dollars.

**Key policy terms (effective 2025-01-01):**
- Maximum LTV: 80%
- Maximum loan term: 30 years
- Minimum credit score: 680
