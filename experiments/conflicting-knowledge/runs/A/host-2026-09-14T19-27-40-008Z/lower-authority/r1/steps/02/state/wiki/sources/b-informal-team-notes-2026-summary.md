These informal team notes (Document ID: B), published 2026-02-01 by the lending operations team, discuss the Northstar Standard Home Loan for owner-occupied purchase loans by standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars — the same product and borrower class covered by the approved policy (Document A). The notes are explicitly informal and not a product of the Lending Policy Committee, nor do they constitute policy approval or a policy change.

The key data point recorded is a maximum LTV of 85%, differing from whatever figure is in the approved policy. No maximum loan term or minimum credit score is stated. Critically, the document contains no language asserting precedence over approved policy, and its publication date is not an effective date for any revised lending rule.

This source serves as a case study in policy governance: it demonstrates a scenario where informal, undocumented team discussion diverges from formal approved policy, and underscores that such notes cannot override or supersede committee-approved documents absent explicit approval and effective-dating.
