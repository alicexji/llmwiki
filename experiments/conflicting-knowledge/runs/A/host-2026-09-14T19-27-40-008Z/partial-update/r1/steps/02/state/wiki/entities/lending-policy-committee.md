The **Lending Policy Committee** is the approving authority for the Northstar Standard Home Loan policy. It approved Document ID A on 2024-12-15, effective 2025-01-01. The committee holds sole authority to approve, amend, or replace lending policy; informal notes or drafts cannot override its decisions. When conflicting equally authoritative requirements arise, resolution is pending further clarification from this committee.
