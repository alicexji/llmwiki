# Atlas Compliance Review

The Compliance team completed its initial Atlas review on September 2.

The review identified three documentation items that needed to be completed before general availability.

Jordan Lee coordinated the review between Compliance and the Atlas Product team.

Two of the documentation items were completed during the following week. The third remained open as of September 12.

The Atlas readiness team continued tracking the remaining item as part of general availability preparation.

Jordan Lee is the Product Manager responsible for coordinating Atlas readiness activities across Product, Engineering, and Compliance.
