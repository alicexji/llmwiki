This document is a brief summary of customer feedback received by the Product team during August regarding Project Atlas, the pilot lending platform. Pilot users reported perceived slowness on certain pages, particularly when loading large loan scenarios, and support ticket volume increased relative to July. The main themes in support tickets were navigation confusion, login difficulties, and slow page loads.

The Product team has asked Engineering to continue monitoring application performance as the pilot expands to more users. Notably, no single root cause has been identified for the rise in support requests, suggesting the issues may stem from multiple contributing factors (e.g., performance, UX, and authentication-related friction) rather than one specific defect.

This feedback aligns with other concurrent issues surfacing in the wider Atlas program, including the September 4 authentication incident and ongoing performance investigations, suggesting a pattern of usability and reliability concerns during the pilot phase.
