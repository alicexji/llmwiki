This short status note clarifies the current availability of Project Atlas: it is limited to approved Northstar Financial employees in an internal pilot program, with no release to external customers. The pilot's purpose is to gather feedback ahead of a broader internal rollout, and external availability has not been approved or scheduled. The document notes that any future external release would require additional product, compliance, and security review, distinguishing the internal pilot phase from any potential external launch.
