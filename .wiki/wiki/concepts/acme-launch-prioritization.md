**Acme Launch Prioritization** refers to the Sales team's recommendation to prioritize the customer Acme during the **Project Atlas** launch planning process.

Key points:
- Acme is considered one of the most strategically important customers for the initial rollout.
- Dedicated onboarding support is recommended to ensure a successful deployment.
- Success with Acme could unlock expansion across multiple business units within the customer's organization.
- The account team will continue rollout planning collaboration with Acme.
