**Maya Chen** is Director of Platform Engineering, leading the organization responsible for developing and operating **Project Atlas**. She participates in Atlas leadership reviews, providing engineering updates to technology leadership, but does not unilaterally make release decisions—these are made collectively through the Atlas readiness and leadership review process.
