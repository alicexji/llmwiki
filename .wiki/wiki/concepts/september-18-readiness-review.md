A scheduled review on September 18 intended to determine Project Atlas's updated launch date. Leadership is withholding a final launch schedule until this review concludes, and the review is closely tied to incorporating recent **security recommendations** into remaining engineering work. Maya Chen is expected to present the readiness update stemming from this review at the next technology leadership meeting.
