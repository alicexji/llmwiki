Official corporate travel policy (Source Type: Corporate Policy; Department: Finance & Corporate Operations; Status: Official Policy; Effective: January 15, 2026) ratified by the Executive Committee. Mandates Economy/Main Cabin for all commercial flights under eight (8) hours globally. Business and First Class bookings under 8 hours are non-reimbursable. Departmental managers lack authority to grant travel variances.
