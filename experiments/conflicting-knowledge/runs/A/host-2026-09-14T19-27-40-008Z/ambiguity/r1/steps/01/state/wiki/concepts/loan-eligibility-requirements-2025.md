Core numeric eligibility criteria set by the 2025 approved policy for Northstar Standard Home Loan:

- **Maximum LTV:** 80%
- **Maximum loan term:** 30 years
- **Minimum credit score:** 680

These apply specifically to owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in Northland.
