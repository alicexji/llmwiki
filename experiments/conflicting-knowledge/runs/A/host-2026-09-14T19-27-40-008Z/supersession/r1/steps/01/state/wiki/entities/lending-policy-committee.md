The **Lending Policy Committee** is the approving authority for Northstar Standard Home Loan policies. It approved Document A (the 2025 policy) on 2024-12-15. Only this committee can approve changes to lending policy; informal notes or draft discussions cannot alter approved policy. When conflicting requirements arise without clear precedence, matters are escalated to this committee for clarification rather than resolved by staff.
