# Northstar Standard Home Loan: draft LTV discussion

Document ID: F. This draft discussion was published by the lending operations team on 2026-02-01. Its status is draft. It has no effective date and does not supersede another document. It is not approved by the Lending Policy Committee and is not an approved policy change.

The discussion is about Northstar Standard Home Loan owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. The team is discussing possibilities for that product rather than documenting a decision for a different established product.

We may be able to go up to 85% LTV for some applications; scope and approval are still to be confirmed. The draft does not identify which applications would qualify. It does not establish that the proposed figure is available to any borrower today.

This document records a tentative discussion. It does not assert a definite replacement for the approved maximum LTV, nor does it define an approved exception. It states no loan term or credit score requirement. Any future policy decision would need its own approval and effective date.
