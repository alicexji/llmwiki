# Northstar Standard Home Loan: approved LTV directive

Document ID: D. This directive was approved by the Lending Policy Committee and published on 2024-12-15. Its status is approved. It takes effect on 2025-01-01. It does not state that it supersedes another document or that it has precedence over another committee-approved policy.

The directive applies to Northstar Standard Home Loan owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. It covers the same product, jurisdiction and borrower class as policy A. It is not limited to an exceptional group of applications.

The maximum loan-to-value ratio (LTV) is 83%. This is an approved requirement stated by the committee in this directive. The directive does not state a maximum loan term or a minimum credit score.

The approving body is the same Lending Policy Committee that approved policy A. No higher authority, later approval date, alternate effective date or special scope is identified here. This directive does not provide a rule for selecting between incompatible requirements stated in documents of equal authority and date.
