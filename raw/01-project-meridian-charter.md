# Project Meridian: Program Charter & Strategic Objectives

Project Meridian represents Aetheris Financial Technologies' flagship modernization initiative for fiscal year 2026, sponsored by VP of Engineering Marcus Vance with product direction from Jordan Hayes and technical architecture led by Elena Rostova. The charter was established in mid-January 2026 for circulation across engineering, product management, and commercial operations.

## Executive Summary
The primary objective of Project Meridian is to replace our fragmented legacy settlement subsystems with a unified, cloud-native real-time transaction engine capable of handling up to 100,000 transactions per second (TPS) with sub-50ms p99 latency.

## Key Stakeholders & Responsibilities
- **Marcus Vance (VP of Engineering):** Executive sponsorship, cross-functional resource allocation, and organizational alignment.
- **Jordan Hayes (Product Lead):** Milestone tracking, client pilot coordination, product roadmap, and go-to-market execution.
- **Elena Rostova (Principal Architect):** End-to-end system architecture, data layer selection, protocol design, and technical governance.
- **Sarah Lin (VP of Enterprise Sales):** Commercial onboarding, partner relationship management, and pilot customer agreements.
- **Devon Thorne (Head of Risk Management):** Transaction underwriting thresholds, credit risk guardrails, and regulatory compliance.

## Target Pilot Partners
The platform will debut with two primary pilot integration partners:
1. **Helix Logistics:** Cross-border freight payment rails and carrier disbursement settlements.
2. **Solis Retail Group:** Multi-channel merchant settlement and instant refund routing.

## Initial Scope & Timeline
- **Q1 2026:** Architecture finalization, data schema modeling, and core ingestion prototype.
- **Q2 2026:** Settlement pipeline buildout, security hardening, and internal load testing.
- **Q3 2026:** Partner sandbox integration, beta trials, and planned General Availability (GA).
