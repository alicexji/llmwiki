Project Meridian is Aetheris Financial Technologies' primary modernization initiative for fiscal year 2026 (charter published January 15, 2026), sponsored by VP of Engineering Marcus Vance with product leadership from Jordan Hayes and principal architecture led by Elena Rostova. The program aims to replace fragmented legacy settlement subsystems with a unified, cloud-native real-time transaction engine targeting 100,000 transactions per second (TPS) with sub-50ms p99 latency.

The platform targets two pilot integration partners: Helix Logistics for cross-border freight payment rails, and Solis Retail Group for multi-channel merchant settlement and instant refund routing. Commercial onboarding is led by Sarah Lin (VP of Enterprise Sales) and risk underwriting guardrails are managed by Devon Thorne (Head of Risk Management).
