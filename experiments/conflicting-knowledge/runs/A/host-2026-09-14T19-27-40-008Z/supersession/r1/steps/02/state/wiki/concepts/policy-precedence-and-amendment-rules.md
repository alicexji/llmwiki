This concept covers the governance logic for how lending policy documents relate to one another:

- **Replacement documents** take effect on their stated effective date, not their publication date.
- **Amendments** only change the specific requirements they name; all other requirements remain in force.
- If two equally authoritative documents with the same date state **incompatible requirements** and neither establishes precedence, the conflict is **unresolved** pending committee clarification.
- Staff must not invent exceptions or choose values to resolve such conflicts on their own.

This framework ensures policy changes are traceable, authoritative, and not subject to informal interpretation.
