This is a draft discussion document (Document ID: F), published 2026-02-01 by the lending operations team, regarding a possible change to the Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio for **Northstar Standard Home Loan** owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in **Northland**. The document explicitly has draft status: it is not approved by the **Lending Policy Committee**, has no effective date, and does not supersede any other document.

The core content floats a possibility of allowing up to 85% LTV for some applications, but the scope, eligibility criteria, and approval process remain unconfirmed. Critically, the document does not identify which applications would qualify, nor does it assert this rate is currently available to any borrower. No loan term or credit score requirements are specified.

The document is notable primarily for what it does *not* do: it does not establish a new approved policy, does not define an approved exception to existing LTV limits, and does not replace the currently approved maximum LTV. Any future policy change based on this discussion would require separate formal approval and an effective date, per standard policy governance rules.
