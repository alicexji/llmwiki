External regulatory compliance notice (Source Type: Regulatory Advisory; Department: Compliance / External Regulatory Liaison; Status: Regulatory Guidance; Published: April 18, 2026) summarizing European Data Protection Board (EDPB) enforcement rulings. Establishes that pseudonymized (salted/hashed) data remains legally personal data under GDPR Article 4(5). Unilateral cross-border replication of European customer telemetry to US servers without Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Transfer Impact Assessments is illegal and must be suspended.
