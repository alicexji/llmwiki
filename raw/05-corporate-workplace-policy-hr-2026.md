# Corporate Workplace & Attendance Policy

Issued by the People & Culture / Human Resources department and formally approved by the Executive Committee for all full-time and part-time employees globally starting January 2026.

## Flexible Working and Hybrid Attendance Guidelines

Aetheris Financial Technologies believes in the value of in-person collaboration, team cohesion, and spontaneous cross-departmental problem solving.

### Hybrid Schedule Mandate
- All full-time employees are required to work on-site at their designated regional corporate office for a minimum of three (3) days per week.
- Remote work is permitted for a maximum of two (2) days per week.
- Departmental managers or team leads do not have the authority to grant permanent or temporary exemptions to this policy.

### Formal Exception Process
Any temporary modification or variance from the mandatory minimum on-site schedule requires:
1. Formal submission of an Accommodation Request Form through the People Portal.
2. Review and endorsement by the Chief People Officer.
3. Final written authorization from the Executive Committee.

Failure to adhere to corporate attendance guidelines may result in corrective action up to and including termination of employment.
