This document is bulletin G, an **approved** communication from the Lending Policy Committee reaffirming the terms of policy A for the Northstar Standard Home Loan. Published and effective 2025-06-01, it does not supersede any prior document and introduces no new requirements—its sole purpose is to restate and confirm existing limits for owner-occupied purchase loans made to standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars.

The bulletin reaffirms three key figures from policy A: a maximum loan-to-value ratio (LTV) of 80%, a maximum loan term of 30 years, and a minimum credit score of 680. It explicitly states these are confirmations, not replacements or new exceptions, and that approval authority remains unchanged.

Staff are instructed that bulletin G and policy A may be cited together as consistent, approved evidence of these requirements, reinforcing document precedence and consistency practices across teams referencing the policy.
