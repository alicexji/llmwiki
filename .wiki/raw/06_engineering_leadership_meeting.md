# Engineering Leadership Meeting Notes

Engineering leadership discussed team scheduling and office attendance for the upcoming quarter.

Several managers reported that flexible scheduling has helped with recruiting and retention. Following the discussion, engineering teams will operate on a three-day remote schedule for the upcoming quarter.

Managers should coordinate schedules within their individual teams to make sure important meetings and collaborative work remain covered. Team leads will communicate the updated arrangement during their next team meetings.

The group plans to revisit the arrangement at the end of the quarter.
