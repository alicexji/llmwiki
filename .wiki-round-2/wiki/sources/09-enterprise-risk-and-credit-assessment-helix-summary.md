Counterparty credit risk assessment published on May 20, 2026 by Devon Thorne, Head of Risk Management. Helix Logistics is classified as elevated counterparty risk due to a 3.8x debt-to-equity ratio, an 18% YoY operating margin compression from fuel price volatility, and an active European customs valuation audit. The Risk Committee mandates a $2.5M cash reserve escrow, a $1.2M daily exposure cap, and strictly rejects any fee waivers or billing concessions.
