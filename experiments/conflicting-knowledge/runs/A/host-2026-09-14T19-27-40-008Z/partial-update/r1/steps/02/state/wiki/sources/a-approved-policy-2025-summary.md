This document defines the **approved 2025 lending policy** (Document ID: A) for the **Northstar Standard Home Loan**, an owner-occupied purchase loan product targeted at standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of **Northland**, denominated in Northland dollars. The policy was approved by the **Lending Policy Committee**, published on 2024-12-15, and carries an effective date of 2025-01-01. It does not supersede any prior document.

Key lending parameters set by this policy are: a maximum **loan-to-value ratio (LTV) of 80%**, a maximum **loan term of 30 years**, and a minimum **credit score of 680**. These requirements apply uniformly to the specified product and borrower class.

The document also establishes governance rules for policy interpretation and change management: only the Lending Policy Committee can approve changes; informal notes or drafts have no authority; replacements take effect on their stated effective date rather than publication date; amendments affect only the specific requirements they name; and in cases of conflicting, equally authoritative requirements with no established precedence, the matter remains unresolved and staff must not improvise a resolution.
