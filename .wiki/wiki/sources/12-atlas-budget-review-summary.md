This document summarizes the Q3 budget review for Project Atlas, noting that overall spending exceeded the quarterly forecast by approximately 8%. The largest cost drivers were engineering labor, cloud infrastructure, and external services, with cloud infrastructure costs rising due to the expansion of the Atlas pilot to more users and environments. Additional planned expenses stemmed from database migration work and performance testing efforts.

Finance and Platform Engineering are jointly reviewing the remaining 2026 forecast in light of this variance. Notably, no single initiative has been identified as the primary driver of the overspend, suggesting the increase is distributed across multiple ongoing workstreams rather than one specific issue.
