This concept covers the rules governing how lending policy documents are approved, amended, replaced, and interpreted:

- Only the approving authority (e.g., Lending Policy Committee) can change approved policy.
- Informal notes/drafts cannot override approved policy.
- Explicit replacements take effect on their **stated effective date**, not publication date.
- Amendments only change the specific requirements they name; other requirements remain in force.
- If equally authoritative documents conflict with no established precedence, the requirement is **unresolved** pending clarification — staff must not invent values or exceptions.
