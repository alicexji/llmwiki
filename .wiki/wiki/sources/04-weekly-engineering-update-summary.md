This weekly engineering update recaps a busy week for the Atlas team, covering three main events: completion of a planned PostgreSQL migration on Monday, a 47-minute production service interruption on Wednesday affecting loan scenario submissions, and ongoing preparation for Atlas's planned October release.

After the migration, engineers reported successful initial validation. Following the incident, the team spent the rest of the week reviewing application logs, database metrics, and service dependencies, though no root cause is stated in this update. Maya thanked the engineering team for responding quickly to the incident.
