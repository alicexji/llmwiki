## Overview
Summary of customer feedback received by the Product team in August regarding **Project Atlas**.

## Key Findings
- Pilot users reported slower page loads during heavy usage periods.
- Delays were specifically noted when loading **large loan scenarios**.
- Support ticket volume increased compared to July.
- Common ticket topics: navigation questions, login difficulties, and slow page loads.
- Product asked Engineering to continue monitoring performance as the pilot expands.
- No single root cause has been identified for the increase in support requests.

## Significance
This feedback highlights emerging usability and performance concerns during the Atlas pilot, overlapping with authentication and performance issues reported elsewhere in the program.
