**Summary:** Project Atlas is currently limited to an internal pilot among approved Northstar Financial employees. It has **not** been released externally, and external release is neither approved nor scheduled.

- Current phase: internal pilot for feedback gathering, ahead of broader internal rollout.
- External customer access: not approved or scheduled.
- Any future external release requires additional **product, compliance, and security review**.

This status clarifies scope for stakeholders evaluating Atlas's readiness relative to GA and external launch discussions.
