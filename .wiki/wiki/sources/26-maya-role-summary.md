This short source document clarifies leadership roles related to Project Atlas. Maya Chen holds the title of Director of Platform Engineering, and her organization includes the engineering team responsible for developing and operating Atlas. She participates in Atlas leadership reviews and provides engineering updates to technology leadership.

The document distinguishes role boundaries: product strategy for Atlas is led by the Product Lending organization, separate from Maya Chen's engineering role. It also clarifies governance: final release decisions for Atlas are made through the Atlas readiness and leadership review process, not by a single individual, correcting any assumption that one person (such as Maya Chen) unilaterally controls release decisions.
