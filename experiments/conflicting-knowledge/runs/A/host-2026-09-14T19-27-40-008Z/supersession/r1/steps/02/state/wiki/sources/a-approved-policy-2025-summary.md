This document defines the **approved 2025 lending policy** (Document ID: A) for the Northstar Standard Home Loan, specifically for owner-occupied purchase loans made to standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. The policy was approved by the **Lending Policy Committee** on 2024-12-15 and became effective 2025-01-01. It does not supersede any prior document.

Key numerical requirements include a **maximum LTV of 80%**, a **maximum loan term of 30 years**, and a **minimum credit score of 680**. These figures apply specifically to the stated product/borrower/jurisdiction combination.

The document also encodes governance rules: only the Lending Policy Committee can approve changes; informal notes cannot override approved policy; replacements take effect on their stated effective date rather than publication date; amendments only change the specific requirements they name; and in cases of conflicting equally-authoritative documents with no established precedence, staff must leave the matter unresolved rather than guessing or inventing exceptions.
