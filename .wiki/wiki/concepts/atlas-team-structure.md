## Overview
Describes the organizational structure supporting **Project Atlas** development.

- **Platform Engineering** (led by **Maya Chen**) is the primary team, including backend, frontend, and site reliability engineers.
- **Data Engineering** supplies shared loan and risk data pipelines.
- **Security Engineering** conducts security reviews and sets requirements.
- **Product Lending** defines product requirements and coordinates the pilot program.
