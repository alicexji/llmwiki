The **Atlas launch timeline** targets an **October 1** customer rollout. Key milestones:
- **Mid-September**: Core platform ready (Engineering)
- **Remaining ~2 weeks**: Final validation and deployment preparation
- **October 1**: Customer launch, supported by Sales communications and Customer Success onboarding

Progress and risks are reviewed in a recurring weekly meeting.
