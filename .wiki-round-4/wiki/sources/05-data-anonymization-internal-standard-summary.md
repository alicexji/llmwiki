Internal technical standard (Source Type: Internal Standard; Department: Data Engineering & Analytics; Status: Approved Internal Standard; Published: January 20, 2026) approved by the Engineering Architecture Council. Defines salted SHA-256 pseudonymization for user IP addresses, tenant IDs, and account numbers. Classifies salted data as de-identified and authorizes cross-region replication from European/APAC worker nodes to central US-East S3 buckets for machine learning training.
