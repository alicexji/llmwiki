This weekly engineering update recaps a busy week for the Atlas team, covering three major threads: the completed PostgreSQL migration, a production incident, and ongoing October release preparation. On Monday, engineering completed the planned MySQL-to-PostgreSQL migration, with initial validation reported successful. On Wednesday, a 47-minute production service interruption affected loan scenario submissions, prompting engineers to spend the rest of the week reviewing application logs, database metrics, and service dependencies to understand root cause.

The update also notes continued work preparing Atlas for its planned October release, indicating the team is balancing incident response and migration follow-up with forward release milestones. Maya (Chen) is noted as thanking the engineering team for their quick incident response, reflecting her leadership role over the Atlas effort.

This document is a short status update rather than a deep dive, but it ties together several other tracked threads: the database migration, the August/September service incidents, and October GA readiness planning.
