Maya Chen is **Director of Platform Engineering**, leading the engineering organization responsible for developing and operating Project Atlas. She participates in Atlas leadership reviews, delivering engineering status updates to technology leadership. Her role is engineering-focused; product strategy responsibility lies with the Product Lending organization, and final release decisions are made collectively through the Atlas readiness review process rather than by Maya alone.
