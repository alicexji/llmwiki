Refers to the schedule slippage experienced during Project Atlas's integration testing phase. The delay stems from problems found in the customer identity migration workflow, requiring roughly three additional weeks for remediation and regression testing before rollout.

This delay pushed the Atlas launch date to **November 1**, though it did not affect the scope of the initial release. Sales and Customer Success teams were asked to adjust communications accordingly.
