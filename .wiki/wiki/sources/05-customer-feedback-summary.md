This source is a short summary of customer feedback related to Project Atlas received in August. The Product team observed an uptick in feedback from pilot users, with several reports of slow page performance during periods of heavy usage — particularly when loading large loan scenarios, a workflow tied to the Commercial Lending Platform. Support ticket volume also rose compared to July, with common themes including navigation confusion, login difficulties, and slow page loads.

Notably, no single root cause has been identified for the rise in support requests, though the login difficulties may be connected to broader authentication issues discussed elsewhere (e.g., the August service incident). Product has asked Engineering to continue monitoring application performance as the Atlas pilot expands, suggesting performance monitoring will be an ongoing priority heading into wider rollout.
