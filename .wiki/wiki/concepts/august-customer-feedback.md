## Overview
During August, the Product team observed an increase in feedback related to the Atlas pilot.

## Key Points
- Pilot users reported slower page performance during heavy usage periods, particularly when loading large loan scenarios.
- Support tickets increased compared to July.
- Common ticket topics: navigation questions, login difficulties, and slow page loads.
- Product asked Engineering to continue monitoring application performance as the pilot expands.
- No single cause has been identified for the increase in support requests.
