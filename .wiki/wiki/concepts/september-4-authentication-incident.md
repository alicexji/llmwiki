## Overview
On September 4, Atlas users experienced intermittent login failures. The Atlas team observed elevated authentication error rates and engaged **AuthCore**, the third-party authentication provider, to investigate.

## Timeline & Resolution
- Elevated auth error rates detected during the incident.
- Service returned to normal later that same morning.

## Root Cause Status
As of this report, the root cause is **undetermined** — it is unclear whether the issue originated in Atlas, in AuthCore, or in another dependency. AuthCore continues to assist Northstar Financial with the investigation.
