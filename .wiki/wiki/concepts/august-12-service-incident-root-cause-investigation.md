## Overview
A production service interruption affecting **Project Atlas** occurred on **August 12** from 2:14 PM to 3:01 PM.

## Impact
- Some users could not submit new loan scenarios.
- Existing saved scenarios remained accessible (partial outage).

## Resolution
**Platform Engineering** restored normal service at 3:01 PM (~47 minute incident duration).

## Status
- Root cause investigation is **still open**.
- No confirmed root cause identified yet.
- Additional monitoring has been enabled pending further investigation.
