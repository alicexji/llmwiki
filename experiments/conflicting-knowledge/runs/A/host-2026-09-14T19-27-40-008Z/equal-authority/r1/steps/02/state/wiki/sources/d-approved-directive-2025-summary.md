This document (Document ID: D) is an approved directive issued by the Lending Policy Committee, published 2024-12-15 and effective 2025-01-01, concerning the Northstar Standard Home Loan product. It sets a maximum loan-to-value ratio (LTV) of 83% for owner-occupied purchase loans made to standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars — the same product, jurisdiction, and borrower class scope as policy A.

Notably, the directive does not state a maximum loan term or minimum credit score, and it does not claim to supersede or take precedence over any other committee-approved policy, including policy A. It was approved by the same committee as policy A, with no higher authority, later approval date, or special scope identified. This creates a potential ambiguity: since both documents cover identical scope and are approved by the same body, but the directive does not provide a tie-breaking rule for conflicting requirements, any discrepancy between this directive and policy A's LTV requirement remains unresolved by explicit precedence rules.

This lack of an explicit precedence mechanism highlights the importance of the general policy precedence and amendment rules used elsewhere in the wiki to adjudicate between equally-authoritative, equally-dated documents that conflict on substantive terms like LTV.
