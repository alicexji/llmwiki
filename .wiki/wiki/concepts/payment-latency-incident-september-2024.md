## Overview
A payment-processing latency incident affecting **Project Atlas**, triggered after a service deployment.

## Timeline
- **Sept 8**: Updated payment-processing service deployed.
- **Sept 10**: Monitoring detected increased average latency during peak usage.
- **Sept 10–12**: Engineering team reviewed logs and infrastructure metrics.
- **Sept 12**: Performance returned to normal after application configuration changes.

## Resolution
No explicit root cause was documented; the issue was mitigated through configuration changes rather than a code rollback.
