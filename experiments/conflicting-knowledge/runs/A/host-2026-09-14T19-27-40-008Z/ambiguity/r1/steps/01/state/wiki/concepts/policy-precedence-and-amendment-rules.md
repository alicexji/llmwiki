Governance rules describing how lending policy documents interact:

- **Replacement documents** take effect on their stated *effective date*, not their publication date.
- **Amendments** change only the specific requirements they name; all other requirements remain in force.
- If two **equally authoritative documents** with the same dates conflict and neither establishes precedence, the requirement is **unresolved** pending committee clarification.
- Staff must **not** invent exceptions or choose values to resolve such conflicts themselves.
