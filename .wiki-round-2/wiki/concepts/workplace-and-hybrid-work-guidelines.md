Guidelines and policies regarding remote work and on-site attendance at Aetheris Financial Technologies.

### Corporate Workplace Policy (Official HR Policy — Effective Date: 2026-01-01)
The official Corporate Workplace & Attendance Policy issued by Human Resources and approved by the Executive Committee (effective January 1, 2026) establishes:
- All full-time employees must work on-site for a minimum of **three (3) days per week**.
- Remote work is limited to a maximum of **two (2) days per week**.
- Departmental managers and team leads are explicitly prohibited from granting exemptions or variances without formal Executive Committee and Chief People Officer approval.

### Engineering Department Stated Norms (All-Hands Sync — Published: 2026-04-10)
During the April 10, 2026 engineering all-hands meeting, VP of Engineering [Marcus Vance](../entities/marcus-vance.md) stated that Project Meridian engineers are permitted to work remotely up to **three (3) days per week** (with Tuesdays and Thursdays on-site).

*Temporal and Authority Evaluation:*
- The HR policy became effective earlier on **January 1, 2026**, establishing a strict 2-day remote ceiling and prohibiting manager-level exemptions.
- The engineering all-hands took place later on **April 10, 2026**, offering a 3-day remote allowance.
- *Unresolved Contradiction:* Because no formal executive exception documentation or authority metadata is present to confirm whether the April 10 verbal statement legally supersedes the January 1 corporate policy, the conflict remains flagged for administrative clarification.
