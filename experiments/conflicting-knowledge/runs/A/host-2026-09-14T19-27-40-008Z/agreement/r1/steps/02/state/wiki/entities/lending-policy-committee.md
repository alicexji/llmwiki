The **approving authority** for Northstar Standard Home Loan policies. Approved Document ID A on 2024-12-15.

Governance role includes:
- Sole body able to approve or replace policy
- Amendments it issues only affect named requirements
- Conflicts between equally authoritative documents require committee clarification; staff cannot resolve these independently
