This short incident report documents a production service interruption for **Project Atlas** that occurred on **August 12** between 2:14 PM and 3:01 PM. During the outage, some users were unable to submit new loan scenarios, though previously saved scenarios remained accessible, indicating a partial rather than full outage.

**Platform Engineering** restored normal service by 3:01 PM, a resolution time of roughly 47 minutes. Notably, the root cause investigation remains open at the time of writing — no confirmed cause has been identified. As a stopgap measure, the team has enabled additional monitoring while the investigation continues, suggesting increased observability was needed to diagnose the issue further.

This document appears closely related to the existing 'August 12 Service Incident' concept page already in the wiki, and provides corroborating or updated detail on the event, particularly emphasizing the unresolved root cause status.
