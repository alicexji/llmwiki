Project Atlas's leadership is divided across functions: **Platform Engineering**, led by Maya Chen, owns engineering development and operations; **Product Lending** leads product strategy. Release decisions are not made unilaterally but through the **Atlas readiness and leadership review process**, a governance mechanism combining input from multiple leaders and functions rather than a single decision-maker.
