# August Customer Feedback Summary

The Product team received an increase in Atlas-related feedback during August.

Several pilot users reported that some pages felt slower during periods of heavy usage. Users specifically mentioned delays when loading large loan scenarios.

The team also received more support tickets than in July. Common topics included navigation questions, login difficulties, and slow page loads.

Product has asked Engineering to continue monitoring application performance as the pilot expands.

No single cause has been identified for the increase in support requests.
