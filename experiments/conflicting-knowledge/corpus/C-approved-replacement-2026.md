# Northstar Standard Home Loan: approved policy 2026

Document ID: C. This replacement policy was approved by the Lending Policy Committee. Its status is approved. It was published on 2026-02-15 and takes effect on 2026-03-01. It explicitly supersedes policy A on 2026-03-01.

This policy applies to Northstar Standard Home Loan owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. The product and borrower scope are unchanged from policy A.

From the effective date, the maximum loan-to-value ratio (LTV) is 82%. The maximum loan term is 30 years. The minimum credit score is 680. These are the approved requirements for this product under this replacement policy.

Publication provides notice of the forthcoming replacement. It does not bring the new requirements into effect early. Policy A continues to govern until the replacement becomes effective. Policy A remains historical evidence after that date and should be identified as the earlier policy when discussing requirements that applied before the replacement. The committee's policy governance rules continue to apply. This document does not approve an informal team statement as policy.
