## Overview
A production service interruption affecting **Project Atlas** occurred on August 12, from **2:14 PM to 3:01 PM** (47 minutes).

## Impact
- Some users were unable to submit new loan scenarios.
- Existing saved scenarios remained accessible, indicating the outage affected submission/write functionality rather than data access.

## Response
- **Platform Engineering** restored normal service at 3:01 PM.
- Root cause investigation remains **open** — no confirmed cause identified at time of reporting.
- Additional monitoring was enabled as a precaution while investigation continues.
