## Overview
A readiness review scheduled for **September 18** to assess whether Project Atlas is on track for its targeted **October general availability (GA)** release.

## Prerequisites for GA
- Security review
- Performance testing
- Operational readiness checks

## Security Recommendations
The Security team provided recommendations on **session management** and **authentication logging**, which Engineering is reviewing as part of readiness. The final GA date will be confirmed after this review.
