## Overview
On **August 18**, the Risk Modeling team released an updated credit-risk model used by **Project Atlas**. The update added new borrower and property characteristics to the risk calculation logic.

## Impact
- Pilot users noticed unexpected changes in their risk scores shortly after the update.
- The Atlas Product team received multiple questions/complaints from pilot users during the following week.

## Resolution
- Product and Risk Modeling teams met on **August 27** to review the feedback.
- Additional documentation explaining model inputs was distributed to pilot users to improve transparency.
