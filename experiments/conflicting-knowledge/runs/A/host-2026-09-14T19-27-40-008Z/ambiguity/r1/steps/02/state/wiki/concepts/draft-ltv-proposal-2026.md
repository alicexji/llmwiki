A tentative, unapproved discussion raised by the lending operations team on 2026-02-01 proposing that **Northstar Standard Home Loan** owner-occupied purchase loans in **Northland** might allow up to **85% LTV** for some applications.

**Status:** Draft only — not approved by the **Lending Policy Committee**, no effective date, does not supersede existing policy, and does not define an approved exception. Scope of qualifying applications is unconfirmed, and no loan term or credit score requirements are specified. This proposal does not currently change borrower eligibility under the approved 2025 loan eligibility requirements.
