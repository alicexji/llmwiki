This source outlines the organizational structure behind Project Atlas. Platform Engineering, led by Director Maya Chen, is the primary engineering organization responsible for Atlas, with an internal development team spanning backend, frontend, and site reliability engineers. Several other teams contribute supporting services rather than owning the project outright: Data Engineering supplies shared loan and risk data pipelines, Security Engineering performs security reviews and defines security requirements, and the Product Lending team sets product requirements and coordinates the pilot program.

The document clarifies role boundaries across teams contributing to Atlas, distinguishing ownership (Platform Engineering) from supporting contributions (Data Engineering, Security Engineering, Product Lending).
