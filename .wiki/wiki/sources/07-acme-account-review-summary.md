This brief document summarizes a Sales team review of the Acme account, conducted as part of planning for the Project Atlas launch. Acme is identified as a strategically important customer for the initial Atlas rollout, with potential to expand usage across multiple business units if the first deployment succeeds.

Sales recommends prioritizing Acme during launch planning and providing dedicated onboarding support, citing a significant expansion opportunity over the following year contingent on a successful deployment. The account team will continue coordinating with Acme on rollout planning.
