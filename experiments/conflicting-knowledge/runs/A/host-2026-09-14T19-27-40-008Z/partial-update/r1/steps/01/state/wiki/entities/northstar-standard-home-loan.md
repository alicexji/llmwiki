The **Northstar Standard Home Loan** is a loan product for owner-occupied purchases by standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. Under the 2025 approved policy (Document ID: A):

- **Maximum LTV**: 80%
- **Maximum loan term**: 30 years
- **Minimum credit score**: 680

These requirements are set by the Lending Policy Committee and took effect 2025-01-01.
