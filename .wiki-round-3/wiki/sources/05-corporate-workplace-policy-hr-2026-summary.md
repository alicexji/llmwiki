Official corporate policy (Source Type: Corporate Policy; Department: Human Resources / Executive Committee; Status: Official Policy; Effective Date: January 1, 2026; Published: January 1, 2026). Mandates all full-time employees globally work on-site a minimum of 3 days per week, restricting remote work to a maximum of 2 days per week. The policy explicitly stipulates that departmental managers lack authority to grant exemptions.
