An **approved amendment** (Document E) to the Northstar Standard Home Loan policy (Policy A), issued by the Lending Policy Committee.

- **Scope**: Changes only the minimum credit score requirement; all other Policy A terms (LTV, max loan term, etc.) remain unchanged.
- **New minimum credit score**: 700
- **Published**: 2026-01-15
- **Effective**: 2026-02-01
- Does not supersede Policy A; must be read together with it.

Illustrates the principle that partial amendments coexist with, rather than replace, the base policy document.
