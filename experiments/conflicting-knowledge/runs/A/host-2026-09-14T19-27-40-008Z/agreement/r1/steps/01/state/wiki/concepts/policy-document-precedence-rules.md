Rules governing how policy documents relate and supersede one another:

- An **explicit replacement** takes effect on its stated effective date, not its publication date.
- An **amendment** changes only the specific requirements it names; all other requirements remain in force.
- If two equally authoritative documents with the same dates conflict and neither establishes precedence, the requirement is **unresolved** pending committee clarification.
- Staff must not invent exceptions or choose values to resolve such conflicts.

This governs how the Lending Policy Committee's documents (like Document A) interact with future policy changes.
