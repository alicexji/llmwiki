The **Northstar Standard Home Loan** is a loan product for owner-occupied purchase loans targeting standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of Northland, denominated in Northland dollars.

Under the 2025 approved policy (Document A):
- Maximum LTV: 80%
- Maximum loan term: 30 years
- Minimum credit score: 680
