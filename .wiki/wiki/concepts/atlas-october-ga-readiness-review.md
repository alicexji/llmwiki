## Overview
A follow-up planning note confirming **Project Atlas** is still targeting **October** for general availability (GA), with an internal readiness review scheduled for **September 18**.

## Requirements Before GA
- Security review
- Performance testing
- Operational readiness checks

## Security Recommendations
The Security team has provided recommendations on **session management** and **authentication logging**, which Engineering is currently reviewing. These likely stem from lessons learned in the September 4 authentication incident.

## Status
The final GA date will be confirmed only after the readiness review is completed.
