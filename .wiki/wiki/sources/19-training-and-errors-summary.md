This brief update reports on Project Atlas pilot expansion in early September, when twenty-five additional users joined after completing a mandatory two-hour training session. During that same week, the Product team observed a rise in incorrectly configured loan scenarios among the newly onboarded users, suggesting the initial training may not have fully prepared them for the platform's scenario configuration workflow.

In response, Product scheduled several additional training sessions for later in September and updated the Atlas user guide to include more examples of scenario configuration. This mirrors themes from earlier documents (e.g., '19_training_and_errors') linking onboarding/training quality to user error rates during the pilot phase.
