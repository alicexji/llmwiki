This document summarizes Product team feedback from August regarding Project Atlas's pilot rollout. Pilot users reported perceived slowdowns on certain pages, particularly when loading large loan scenarios, consistent with performance concerns raised elsewhere in the Atlas program. Support ticket volume increased compared to July, with common themes including navigation confusion, login difficulties, and slow page loads.

Product has requested that Engineering continue monitoring application performance as the pilot expands to more users. Notably, no single root cause has been identified for the rise in support requests, suggesting the issues may stem from multiple contributing factors (e.g., UI/navigation design, authentication flows, and backend performance) rather than one discrete defect.
