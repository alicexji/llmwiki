The **Acme Account Review** is an assessment conducted by the Sales team to evaluate the Acme customer relationship in preparation for the Atlas launch. Key points include:

- Acme is considered one of the most strategically important customers for the initial Atlas rollout
- Interest expressed in expanding usage across multiple business units if the first deployment succeeds
- Recommendation to prioritize Acme and provide dedicated onboarding support
- Potential for significant expansion opportunity over the next year

The account team will continue working with Acme on rollout planning, tying this review closely to the broader Atlas launch timeline.
