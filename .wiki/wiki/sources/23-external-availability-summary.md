This short status note clarifies the current availability of Project Atlas, confirming it is restricted to approved Northstar Financial employees as part of an internal pilot program. The document explicitly states that Atlas has **not** been released to external customers, and no external release has been approved or scheduled.

The internal pilot is described as a mechanism for gathering feedback ahead of a broader internal rollout, rather than a step toward immediate external launch. The document notes that any future external release would require additional product, compliance, and security review, signaling that further gating processes are expected before customer-facing availability.
