Legal directive (Source Type: Legal Guidance; Department: Legal & Regulatory Affairs; Status: Approved; Published: March 5, 2026) issued by the General Counsel. Establishes mandatory statutory preservation obligations under commercial trade and consumer warranty regulations. Requires all customer interactions, support tickets, electronic chat records, and incident reports to be preserved in tamper-evident formats for a minimum of three (3) years (36 months). Deletion without legal concurrence is prohibited.
