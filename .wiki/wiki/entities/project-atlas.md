**Project Atlas** is an internal initiative preparing for a customer rollout targeted for **October 1**. Engineering is responsible for delivering the core platform by mid-September, with the remaining time allocated to validation and deployment preparation. Sales and Customer Success are coordinating communications and onboarding resources around the launch date. Progress is tracked via a recurring weekly Atlas meeting.
