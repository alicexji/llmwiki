## Overview
A brief performance incident affecting the **Atlas** payment-processing service.

- **Sept 8:** Updated payment-processing service deployed.
- **Sept 10:** Monitoring showed increased average latency during peak usage periods.
- **Sept 10–12:** Engineering reviewed service logs and infrastructure metrics.
- **Sept 12:** Performance returned to typical levels after application configuration changes.

No specific root cause was documented beyond the resolving configuration changes.
