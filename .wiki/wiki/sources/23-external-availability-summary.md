This is a brief status note clarifying the availability scope of Project Atlas. It confirms that Atlas remains restricted to approved Northstar Financial employees participating in the internal pilot program, and that it has not been released to any external customers. The document positions the current pilot as a feedback-gathering phase intended to precede a broader internal rollout, rather than a step toward immediate external release.

Notably, the document explicitly states that external customer availability has not been approved or scheduled, and that any future external release would require additional product, compliance, and security review. This suggests governance gates beyond engineering readiness are a prerequisite for external launch, tying into broader themes of compliance and security review seen elsewhere in the Atlas program.
