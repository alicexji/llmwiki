# Northstar Standard Home Loan: approved policy 2025

Document ID: A. This policy was approved by the Lending Policy Committee and published on 2024-12-15. Its status is approved and its effective date is 2025-01-01. It does not supersede another document.

This policy applies to Northstar Standard Home Loan owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. All requirements below apply to that same product and borrower class.

The maximum loan-to-value ratio (LTV) is 80%. The maximum loan term is 30 years. The minimum credit score is 680.

The Lending Policy Committee is the approving authority for this product. Informal team notes and draft discussions cannot change an approved policy. An explicit replacement takes effect on its stated effective date, not its publication date. An amendment changes only the requirements it names; other requirements remain in force. If equally authoritative documents with the same dates state incompatible requirements and neither establishes precedence, the requirement remains unresolved pending committee clarification. Staff must not choose a value or invent an exception to resolve that situation.
