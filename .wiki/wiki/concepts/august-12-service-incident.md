## Overview
A production service interruption affecting **Project Atlas** on August 12, from 2:14 PM to 3:01 PM (47 minutes).

## Impact
- Some users were unable to submit **new** loan scenarios.
- Existing saved scenarios remained accessible throughout.

## Resolution
- **Platform Engineering** restored normal service at 3:01 PM.
- Root cause has **not** been confirmed; investigation remains open.
- Additional monitoring was enabled as a precaution during ongoing investigation.
