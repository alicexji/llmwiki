This document outlines the company's hybrid work policy, allowing employees to work remotely up to two days per week as part of a standard schedule. Teams are given flexibility to coordinate which specific days are remote, but managers retain authority to require onsite presence based on operational or coverage needs.

The policy explicitly restricts department managers from unilaterally increasing the number of remote days beyond the two-day limit; any changes must go through a formal workplace policy approval process. Questions or exceptions are directed to Human Resources, indicating a centralized governance model for workplace flexibility policies.

This document appears unrelated to Project Atlas and instead represents general HR/workplace policy guidance applicable across the organization.
