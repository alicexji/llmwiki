This brief planning note reaffirms that **Project Atlas** is targeting an **October general availability (GA)** launch, with an internal readiness review scheduled for **September 18**. It outlines three prerequisite workstreams that must be completed before GA: security review, performance testing, and operational readiness checks.

The document also notes that the **Security team** recently issued recommendations concerning session management and authentication logging, which Engineering is currently reviewing. The final GA date remains contingent on the outcome of the September 18 readiness review, indicating that the October target is provisional rather than confirmed.
