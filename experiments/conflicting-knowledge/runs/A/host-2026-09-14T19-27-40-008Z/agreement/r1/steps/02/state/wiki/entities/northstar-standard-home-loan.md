A loan product for **owner-occupied purchase loans** targeting standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars.

**2025 Approved Policy Terms:**
- Maximum LTV: 80%
- Maximum loan term: 30 years
- Minimum credit score: 680

Governed by policy Document ID A, approved 2024-12-15, effective 2025-01-01.
