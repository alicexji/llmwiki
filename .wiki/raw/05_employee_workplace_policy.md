# Employee Workplace Policy

Employees participating in the company's hybrid work program may work remotely up to two days during a standard work week.

Teams may coordinate which days employees work remotely based on operational needs. Managers are responsible for ensuring adequate coverage and may require employees to work onsite when business needs require it.

Changes to the number of remote days available under the hybrid work program must be approved through the company's workplace policy process. Individual department managers may not independently expand the number of remote days provided by this policy.

Questions regarding the hybrid work program should be directed to Human Resources.
