The **Atlas Launch Timeline** targets an **October 1** customer rollout:

- **Mid-September**: Core platform ready (Engineering)
- **Mid-Sept to Oct 1**: Final validation and deployment preparation (~2 weeks)
- **Around October 1**: Sales begins customer communications; Customer Success prepares onboarding resources

Progress is tracked in a recurring weekly Atlas meeting where schedule concerns are raised.
