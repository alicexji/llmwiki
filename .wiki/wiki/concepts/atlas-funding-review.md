A 2026 funding review for **Project Atlas** conducted jointly by **Finance** and **Platform Engineering**. The review confirms that current funding is sufficient to continue planned development and pilot activities through the present quarter. No specific funding amounts are disclosed in this update.

A separate 2027 funding request will be reviewed by leadership as part of the annual planning process; a final 2027 budget has not yet been approved.
