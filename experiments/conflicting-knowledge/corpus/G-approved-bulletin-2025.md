# Northstar Standard Home Loan: approved policy bulletin

Document ID: G. This bulletin was approved by the Lending Policy Committee. Its status is approved. It was published on 2025-06-01 and is effective from 2025-06-01. It does not supersede another document.

This bulletin concerns Northstar Standard Home Loan owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in Northland, denominated in Northland dollars. It addresses the same product, jurisdiction and borrower class as policy A.

The committee reaffirms the requirements in policy A: maximum loan-to-value ratio (LTV) is 80%, maximum loan term is 30 years, and minimum credit score is 680. This bulletin confirms those requirements rather than replacing policy A or establishing a different set of limits. There is no change to the scope of the policy.

Staff preparing product information may refer to this bulletin and policy A together as approved evidence of these requirements. Publication of this bulletin does not signal a new exception or a change in approval authority. Its purpose is to restate the applicable figures consistently for teams using the policy.
