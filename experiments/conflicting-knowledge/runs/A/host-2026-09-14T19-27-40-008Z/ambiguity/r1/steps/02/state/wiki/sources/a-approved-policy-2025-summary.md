This document defines the **approved 2025 lending policy** (Document ID: A) for the **Northstar Standard Home Loan** product, targeting owner-occupied purchase loans for standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of **Northland**, denominated in Northland dollars. It was approved by the **Lending Policy Committee** on 2024-12-15 and takes effect 2025-01-01, superseding no prior document.

Key numeric requirements include a maximum **loan-to-value ratio (LTV) of 80%**, a maximum **loan term of 30 years**, and a minimum **credit score of 680**. These apply uniformly to the specified product and borrower class.

The policy also establishes governance rules: only the Lending Policy Committee can approve or change policy; informal notes/drafts have no authority; replacements take effect on their stated effective date (not publication date); amendments affect only the named requirements, leaving others intact; and in cases of conflicting equally authoritative requirements with no established precedence, the matter remains unresolved and staff must not improvise a resolution.
