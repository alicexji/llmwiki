This document outlines the company's **Employee Workplace Policy**, focused on the hybrid work program. Employees are permitted to work remotely up to two days per standard work week, with teams coordinating specific remote days based on operational needs.

Managers hold responsibility for ensuring adequate onsite coverage and can require employees to work onsite when business needs dictate, even on days that might otherwise be remote. Importantly, any changes to the number of allowed remote days must go through the formal company workplace policy process — individual department managers **cannot** unilaterally expand remote day allowances beyond the two-day limit.

The policy directs employees with questions to Human Resources, positioning HR as the authoritative body for policy interpretation and clarification. This document appears unrelated to the Project Atlas technical sources in the wiki, representing a distinct HR/administrative policy area.
