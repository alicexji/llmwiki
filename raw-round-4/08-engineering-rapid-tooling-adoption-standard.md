# Technical Governance Standard: Accelerated Developer Tooling & Infrastructure Sourcing (ENG-GOV-2026-05)

Approved by the Engineering Architecture Council and the Vice President of Engineering, governing developer productivity, toolchain agility, and cloud developer platforms.

## 1. Engineering Agility & Developer Velocity
In high-velocity software engineering domains, standard multi-month administrative sourcing cycles introduce severe platform delivery bottlenecks and delay critical product shipping milestones.

## 2. Fast-Track Tooling Authority
- **Autonomous Sourcing Threshold:** Engineering leads and Technical Directors are authorized to directly select and contract mission-critical developer tools, CI/CD pipeline infrastructure, and observability platforms up to **one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000 USD)** in annual spend.
- **Approval Mechanism:** Fast-track procurement under this standard requires only the review and affirmative vote of two Engineering Architecture Council members, bypassing multi-vendor RFP competitive bidding.
- **Sole-Source Justification:** Standardized tooling selection may be justified on technical architectural compatibility, API ergonomics, or native IDE integration without soliciting third-party alternative tenders.
