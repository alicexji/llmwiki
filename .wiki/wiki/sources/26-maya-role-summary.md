This short document clarifies leadership roles and decision-making structure for Project Atlas. Maya Chen holds the title of Director of Platform Engineering, and her organization is responsible for building and operating Atlas. She participates in leadership reviews, providing engineering status updates to technology leadership.

The document also clarifies organizational boundaries: product strategy for Atlas is owned by the Product Lending organization, distinct from Platform Engineering's technical execution role. Notably, final release decisions are explicitly not made by a single individual but through the collective Atlas readiness and leadership review process, emphasizing a governance-based rather than individual-driven decision model.
