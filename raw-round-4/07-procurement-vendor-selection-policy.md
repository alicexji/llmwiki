# Global Procurement Policy: Software, SaaS, and Vendor Engagement (PROC-POL-2026-03)

Issued by the Global Procurement and Strategic Sourcing Office, governing all commercial contracting and third-party supplier commitments across the enterprise.

## 1. Commercial Governance & Fiduciary Oversight
To ensure competitive commercial pricing, prevent vendor lock-in, and maintain robust financial auditing standards, all software licenses, cloud developer tooling, and SaaS platforms are subject to standardized procurement review thresholds.

## 2. Competitive Bidding & Sourcing Thresholds
- **Threshold for Competitive Tender:** Any third-party software contract, developer tool subscription, or vendor engagement with projected annual spend exceeding **fifty thousand dollars ($50,000 USD)** must undergo a mandatory competitive Request for Proposal (RFP) process.
- **Minimum Bid Requirement:** Procurement requires submissions from a minimum of **three (3) qualified independent vendors** prior to contract award.
- **Review Cycle:** Sourcing reviews require a minimum thirty (30) business day evaluation window for commercial and contractual negotiations.
- **Prohibition on Departmental Sole-Sourcing:** Technical or operational departments may not bypass competitive bidding by entering direct commercial agreements above the $50,000 threshold.
