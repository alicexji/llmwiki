# Legal Advisory & Statutory Preservation Directive: Customer Communication Records (LEG-ADV-2026-14)

Issued by the Office of the General Counsel and Regulatory Affairs Directorate for distribution to Product Management, Engineering Leads, and Corporate Governance.

## 1. Statutory Context & Legal Obligations
Under international commercial trade regulations, consumer contract warranty laws, and active cross-jurisdictional evidentiary discovery rules governing financial software providers, Aetheris Technologies is subject to strict statutory record preservation requirements.

## 2. Mandatory Preservation Requirements
To protect the enterprise against commercial litigation, contract dispute liability, and regulatory non-compliance sanctions:
- **Mandatory Preservation Period:** All records of customer interactions, including technical support transcripts, electronic chat communications, contract negotiation logs, and customer-submitted incident reports, must be preserved for a minimum of **three (3) years (36 months)** from the date of creation.
- **Evidentiary Integrity:** Preserved records must be stored in tamper-evident, append-only formats capable of satisfying legal discovery chain-of-custody standards.
- **Litigation Hold Scope:** Deletion or routine automated purging of any customer dialogue or dispute communication without prior written concurrence from the Legal Department exposes the organization to spoliation penalties.
