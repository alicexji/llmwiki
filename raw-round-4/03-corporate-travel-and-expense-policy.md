# Corporate Travel, Lodging, and Expense Policy (FIN-POL-2026-01)

Issued by the Finance and Corporate Operations Directorate and formally ratified by the Executive Committee, governing all travel expenditures across all business units globally.

## 1. Core Principles & Travel Cost Management
Aetheris Technologies is committed to prudent financial management, carbon footprint reduction, and equitable travel standards across all functional divisions.

## 2. Commercial Airfare Class of Service
- **Standard Domestic & Short-Haul Flights:** All commercial flights with scheduled airborne duration under **eight (8) hours** must be booked in **Economy / Main Cabin** class.
- **Prohibition on Premium Cabin Bookings:** Business Class and First Class airfare bookings are strictly non-reimbursable for flights under eight hours.
- **Expense Compliance:** Expense reimbursement reports containing unapproved premium cabin fares will be automatically rejected by the automated accounts payable system.
- **Departmental Variance Restriction:** Division vice presidents and departmental managers possess no authority to authorize local deviations from standard economy airfare bookings.
