**Project Atlas** is Northstar Financial's initiative to modernize its commercial lending platform, begun in January 2026.

- Led by the Platform Engineering team under Maya Chen
- Aims to replace legacy lending services with a unified platform for loan processing, pricing, and risk analysis
- Target: internal general availability in October 2026
- Depends on Data Engineering pipelines and AuthCore authentication services
