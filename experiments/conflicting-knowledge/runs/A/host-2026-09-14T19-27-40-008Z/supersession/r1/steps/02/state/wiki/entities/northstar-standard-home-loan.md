The **Northstar Standard Home Loan** is a loan product governed by policy Document A, effective 2025-01-01. It applies to **owner-occupied purchase loans** for standard individual borrowers in the fictional jurisdiction of **Northland**, denominated in Northland dollars.

Key approved terms:
- Maximum LTV: **80%**
- Maximum loan term: **30 years**
- Minimum credit score: **680**
