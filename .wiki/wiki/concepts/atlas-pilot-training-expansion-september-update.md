## Overview
In the first week of September, **25 additional users** joined the **Atlas** pilot program, each completing a two-hour training session prior to receiving platform access.

## Issue Identified
During that same week, the Product team observed an **increase in incorrectly configured loan scenarios** among pilot users, suggesting the training may not have fully prepared new users for scenario configuration.

## Response
- Additional training sessions scheduled for later in September.
- The Atlas user guide was updated with more examples of scenario configuration to reduce errors.

This update is closely related to the broader **September Pilot Training Expansion** and **Atlas Pilot Adoption** efforts.
